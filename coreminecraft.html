Understanding Your HUD (Screen Bars)

What the icons at the bottom of your screen mean in Survival mode:

❤️ Health Bar (10 Red Hearts)

Located above the left side of your hotbar. Taking fall damage, monster hits, or drowning drains hearts. If all 10 hearts empty, you die and drop your items at that spot.

🍖 Hunger Bar (10 Drumsticks)

Located on the right side. Hold Right-Click with food in your hand to eat. Crucial Rule: When you have at least 9 drumsticks full, your Hearts heal automatically! Below 3 drumsticks, you cannot sprint.

🛡️ Armor & Hotbar (Slots 1–9)

Use your mouse scroll wheel or keys 1–9 to switch between the 9 items in your bottom hotbar. Equip armor inside your inventory (E) to reduce damage.

Step 01 • Survival Walkthrough

Surviving Your First Day (20 Minutes)

A Minecraft day lasts 10 real-world minutes before sunset. Follow these 6 steps in order.

DAY 1 GUIDE
→ →
Step 1 · Morning

Punch Trees for Wood

Walk up to any tree and hold Left-Click with your bare hand until the log pops out. Collect 5 to 8 Wood Logs.

How to make a Crafting Table

Press E to open your inventory. Place 1 Log into the 2×2 crafting grid to get 4 Wood Planks. Fill all 4 slots of the 2×2 grid with Planks to create a Crafting Table.

→ →
Step 2 · Mid-Morning

Craft a Pickaxe & Mine Stone

Right-click the ground to place your Crafting Table, then Right-click it to open the 3×3 grid. Craft Sticks and a Wooden Pickaxe.

Why only craft one wooden tool?

Wooden tools are slow and weak. Use your Wooden Pickaxe to mine 15–20 gray Stone blocks (from a hillside or shallow dig), then immediately craft a Stone Pickaxe, Stone Axe, and Stone Sword!

→ →
Step 3 · Noon

Find Coal & Make Torches

Look at rocky cliffs or surface caves for gray stone speckled with black spots—that is Coal Ore. Mine it with your Pickaxe.

What if I cannot find Coal?

Craft a Furnace using 8 Cobblestone. Put Wood Logs in the top slot and Wood Planks in the bottom fuel slot to smelt Charcoal. Both Coal and Charcoal craft Torches when placed above a Stick!

→
Step 4 · Afternoon

Gather 3 Wool & Raw Food

While exploring in daylight, look for 3 Sheep of the same color to get 3 Wool blocks, plus Cows, Pigs, or Chickens for meat.

Always cook your meat!

Cooked meat restores 3× to 4× more hunger than raw meat. Put raw beef, porkchops, or mutton into your Furnace with coal or wood planks to cook it.

Step 5 · Before Sunset

Build a Starter Shelter

Before the sun touches the horizon, build a small enclosed room (at least 3 blocks tall with a roof) or hollow out a small room in the side of a hill.

Essential shelter checklist

Craft a Wooden Door so you can walk in and out while keeping monsters out. Bring your Crafting Table and Furnace inside, and place Torches on the walls so monsters cannot spawn inside.

→
Step 6 · Nightfall

Craft a Bed & Skip the Night

Combine 3 matching Wool (middle row) and 3 Wood Planks (bottom row) in your Crafting Table to craft a Bed.

What if I didn't find 3 Sheep?

Stay inside your lit shelter! Spend the 7-minute night smelting food in your Furnace, organizing a Chest, or digging a staircase mine inside your shelter floor. Never break your bed once placed—it saves your respawn location!

Step 03 • Underground Guide

Tool Tiers & Beginner Ores

In Minecraft, mining an ore with the wrong pickaxe destroys the block without dropping anything! Follow this exact upgrade ladder.

Tier 1 • Starter
Wooden Pickaxe

Can Mine: Stone, Cobblestone, and Coal Ore.
Warning: Do NOT mine Iron Ore with a wooden pickaxe—it will break nothing! Upgrade to Stone immediately.

Tier 2 • Early Game
Stone Pickaxe

Can Mine: Iron Ore, Copper Ore, and Lapis Lazuli.
Tip: Cheap and easy to repair. Always carry 2 Stone Pickaxes when exploring caves.

Tier 3 • Main Goal
Iron Pickaxe

Can Mine: Diamonds, Gold Ore, Redstone, and Emeralds.
How to get: Smelt Raw Iron in a Furnace with Coal to get Iron Ingots.

Tier 4 • Advanced
Diamond Pickaxe

Can Mine: Everything, including Obsidian.
Where to find Diamonds: Deep underground in Deepslate layers (below Y = 16, best at Y = -58).

Coal Ore (Fuel & Light)

Drops Coal directly when mined with any pickaxe. Found abundantly in cliffs, hills, and surface caves between Y = 0 and Y = 256 (most common around Y = 96

Iron Ore (Your #1 Priority)

Requires a Stone Pickaxe or better. Drops Raw Iron which you must smelt in a Furnace. Very common in shallow caves around Y = 16 and high mountain peaks.

Match the Tool to the Block

Pickaxe: Stone, Ores, Furnace.
Axe: Logs, Planks, Chests, Crafting Tables.
Shovel: Dirt, Grass, Sand, Gravel.
Hoe: Tilling dirt for farming crops.

Step 04 • Creature Field Guide

Mobs Every Beginner Will Meet

Friendly animals spawn in grassy daylight. Hostile monsters spawn in the dark or at night.

💥 Creeper (Hostile)

Silent, green, four-legged monster that does not burn in sunlight. Walks up to you, hisses, flashes, and explodes! How to fight: Hit once with your sword, immediately sprint backward so the timer resets, then repeat.

🧟 Zombie (Hostile)

Slow-moving melee monster that groans and walks toward you. Burns in morning sunlight unless standing in water or shade. Watch out for Baby Zombies—they run very fast!

🏹 Skeleton (Hostile)

Shoots arrows from a distance and rattles when walking. How to fight: Hold Right-Click with a Shield to bounce their arrows harmlessly away, then rush in between shots. Drops Bones and Arrows.

🕷️ Spider (Hostile at Night)

Can climb up vertical walls! Hostile in the dark, but becomes peaceful during bright daylight unless you hit it first. Drops String (used to craft a Bow, Fishing Rod, or Wool).

👁️ Enderman (Neutral)

Tall black creature with glowing purple eyes that teleports. Golden Rule: Never put your crosshair directly on its face! It is completely peaceful unless you look at its head or attack it.

🐑 Sheep (Friendly)

Gives Wool for your Bed and Raw Mutton for food. Once you have 2 Iron Ingots, craft Shears to harvest 1–3 Wool per sheep without hurting them! Sheep regrow wool after eating grass.

🐄 Cows, Pigs & Chickens

Harmless animals that provide your best early food. Cows drop Raw Beef and Leather. Lead them into a fenced pen by holding Wheat (for Cows/Sheep) or Seeds (for Chickens).

🧑‍🌾 Villager (Friendly)

Found in NPC Villages with Hay Bales, farms, and houses. Right-click them to trade items for Emeralds. Do not hit Villagers or their giant protector, the Iron Golem!

Step 05 • Sustainable Survival

Beginner Farming & 6 Golden Rules

Stop running out of food and avoid the classic mistakes that get every new player killed.

How to Start a Simple Wheat Farm

Never go hungry again with this 4-step starter farm:

1. Break Tall Grass for Seeds

Punch short grass on the ground with your hand to collect green Wheat Seeds

2. Till Dirt Near Water with a Hoe

Craft a Wooden or Stone Hoe (2 Material + 2 Sticks). Right-click Dirt or Grass blocks within 4 blocks of water (a river, pond, or 1 bucket of water) to turn it into dark, wet Farmland.

3. Plant, Wait & Craft Bread

Right-click Farmland with Seeds. Wait until the stalks turn golden-brown at the top before harvesting! Place 3 Wheat in a horizontal row in your Crafting Table to make Bread

The 6 Golden Rules of Minecraft

  • Rule #1: Never dig straight down! You can fall directly into a hidden lava pool or a deep ravine. Dig a 2-block-wide staircase instead so you can always see the block below you.
  • Rule #2: Never dig straight up! Overhead Lava, Gravel, or Sand can fall on your head and suffocate you. Place a Torch at your feet when mining upward.
  • Rule #3: Hold Shift near steep drops! Holding Left Shift locks your feet to the block you are standing on—making it impossible to walk off a cliff or bridge.
  • Rule #4: Java Edition Combat Cooldown: Do not spam-click your sword or axe! Wait half a second for the tiny sword meter under your crosshair to refill between swings for 100% damage.
  • Rule #5: Write down your Base Coordinates (F3): Press F3 and look for XYZ on the left side. Jot down the X and Z numbers of your house so you never get lost.
  • Rule #6: Always carry a Water Bucket: Pour water over yourself if you catch fire, or pour it onto red-hot Lava to turn it into safe, solid Stone and Obsidian.

Pro Tip for Java Beginners: Click the green Recipe Book icon inside your Inventory (E) or Crafting Table at any time in-game! It automatically unlocks and fills in recipes as you pick up new materials.
