Ancient Investigation Journal: Part II

The expedition cannot open the circular chamber and returns without the promised frescoes or weapons. Missing members, a cave-in that kills Nick, and dwindling supplies push the survivors toward a cellar.

Back from the locked chamber

Part II continues the expedition after the attempt to open the circular chamber fails. The team returns to South Base Camp without seeing the frescoes or ancient Starsilver weapons Master Ingbert had hoped to find. The author fears that their earlier notes have been lost as well.

The entry preserves a setback, not a new discovery about Dragonspine’s ancient kingdom. The expedition has not reached the chamber’s contents.

Missing people and dwindling supplies

No one has heard from the companions who disappeared in the snowstorm. The writer hopes they reached the foot of the mountain and can return with help, but the remaining supplies are nearly exhausted.

A cave-in before the chamber killed Nick and buried the food and fuel he carried. The author recalls having warned that the ruins’ structure should be checked first and wonders whether repeated losses are making them bitter.

Eberhart and Landrich

Despite the failures, the writer admires Eberhart’s composure and calls him capable of the role of an aristocrat, even though he is illegitimate. The entry says Landrich’s judgment of him seemed sound, and the author is anxious not to disappoint Landrich.

The journal also hints at the danger of the Lawrence Clan, whose red-haired reaper the writer fears. These lines place the expedition inside a conflict among Mondstadt’s aristocrats; they are the author’s fears and opinions, not an impartial account of every clan’s plans.

The cellar plan

When the storm eases, the survivors intend to inspect a cellar in the southwestern ruins, following a proposal from Eberhart. The writer says Eberhart has deciphered material that suggests something ancient may be inside.

This change returns the party to its original target, but Part II ends before they investigate it. Part III records what happens when Eberhart, Ingbert, and the journal’s author enter that cellar.

Location and record context

Part II is in a chest beneath the ice-lake puzzle near Starglow Cavern. Together with Parts I and III, it is one of the journals Stevens asks the Traveler to collect for A Land Entombed.

The journal describes an expedition’s human losses and changing plan. It does not describe finding the roots of Sal Vindagnyr’s tree, deciphering star murals, or discovering an elemental curse.
