Ancient Investigation Journal: Part III

The final entry reveals the writer’s suspicion that Eberhart lured Master Ingbert into a cellar and attacked him. Badly wounded, the writer leaves a warning for Landrich and does not confirm what followed.

The writer’s final entry

Part III is the last of the three Dragonspine journals. The author writes while gravely injured and cold, apologizes to Priscilla, and says they will not make it back to Mondstadt. The entry is found on a shelf in a cellar reached from Starglow Cavern.

The writer asks whoever finds the page to warn Landrich. This is the point where the expedition record becomes evidence of a personal betrayal as well as a failed archaeological search.

Eberhart’s deception

The author realizes that Eberhart’s talk of a lost civilization and a power from the heavens was a way to get Master Ingbert into an isolated cellar. Eberhart draws a polearm and strikes Ingbert before badly injuring the journal’s writer.

The writer believes Eberhart wanted to clear obstacles on his way to power within the clan, and wonders whether he had encouraged Parsifal’s earlier departure. These are the injured witness’s conclusions; the journal does not independently prove the entire conspiracy or Eberhart’s ultimate objective.

A warning about the succession

The author connects Eberhart’s possible ambition to his status as Landrich’s illegitimate child and to Landrich’s plan to grant him the family name after the expedition. The warning urges Landrich to take the danger seriously if Eberhart returns.

The journal does not say whether Landrich received this warning. It also leaves the writer’s and Ingbert’s fates at the scene; no rescue or return is recorded in this final page.

What Stevens can infer

After the Traveler brings him all three journals, Stevens says the historical sources barely mention the aristocrats’ investigation and provide no account of its return. He thinks the evidence suggests that no member came back, but allows that Eberhart may have escaped.

Stevens has no answer for where Eberhart went, and the quest ends with that question open. The game has not confirmed his eventual fate through these journals.

What the expedition did not find

The journals say that Eberhart promised ancient frescoes and Starsilver weapons in the locked chamber, but the team fails to open it. The final betrayal takes place in a cellar, not in a sealed summit vault, and the pages contain no warning about a divine strike destroying Dragonspine.

The quest makes the journals valuable because they expose a gap in Mondstadt’s political history. It does not connect them to Sal Vindagnyr’s records or to the Snow-Tombed Starsilver weapon.
