First Apprentice of Guhua

A quest-item novel by Chang the Ninth, acquired by peeking inside a sealed package during the Liyue commission This Novel Is Amazing! Its apprentice story is fiction, not a Guhua historical chronicle.

A novel inside a commission package

First Apprentice of Guhua is a quest-item novel by Chang the Ninth, not an Archive volume. During the Liyue commission This Novel Is Amazing!, the Traveler can peek inside Chang's sealed delivery package to read it; the item disappears after the commission or daily reset.

The item description calls it a novel by Chang. That framing is important: the Guhua school and apprentice in the story belong to his fiction.

The frail student and the declining school

The story follows a frail boy adopted by a Guhua Master from the mountains. Though he cannot practice the Guhua arts at first, he grows up in the school and later becomes one of its last two disciples alongside an elderly caretaker.

After the master's death, the boy studies the school's hidden books and techniques, runs the school, and defeats challengers without a Vision or arcane weapon. The text presents this as a fictional martial adventure, not a documented account of Guhua history.

A student from Inazuma

The young man takes a girl from Inazuma as his student. When she leaves without saying goodbye during a turbulent period, he decides to leave the mountain and travel to Inazuma himself.

The novel does not give either character a personal name. Its ending sets up a journey beyond the mountain, but the quest item contains no sequel or confirmation that the fictional trip happened.

Chang the Ninth and Yae Publishing House

A separate Yae Publishing House dialogue identifies Chang the Ninth as the author and says First Apprentice of Guhua won the publisher's This Novel Is Amazing! contest. The commission reveals the story itself; the Inazuma dialogue adds its publication history.

Chang's authorship links the item to a real writer in Liyue, but it does not turn the novel's Guhua Master, apprentice, or events into historical people and events.

What the item establishes

The game establishes a novel by Chang the Ninth, its commission-based reading route, its plot about a Guhua apprentice and an Inazuman student, and its contest success at Yae Publishing House.

It does not establish an ancient first apprentice, the founding principles of a Guhua Brotherhood, or Lightpiercer and Raincutter as weapons in this novel. Treat the plot as Chang's fiction unless another source independently confirms a detail.
