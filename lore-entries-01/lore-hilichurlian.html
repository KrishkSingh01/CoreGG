A constructed language

Hilichurlian is a constructed language used by hilichurls in Genshin Impact. The game gives words and phrases in dialogue, songs, and books, and some quests let the Traveler try to communicate with hilichurls.

The language has patterns beyond a list of funny words. Researchers in the game look at vocabulary, grammar, and context, while players compare translations with the scenes in which a phrase appears.

Ella Musk’s research

Ella Musk studies Hilichurlian and asks the Traveler to help her approach hilichurls peacefully. Her commissions show both the promise and the difficulty of translation: a phrase can be misunderstood when the speaker’s context is missing.

The Handy Handbook of Hilichurlian and Hilichurl Ballad Selection provide written examples. The books are in-world sources and may reflect the author’s limits as well as the language itself.

Words need context

A single translated word does not always tell the whole meaning of a line. Tone, gesture, repetition, and the situation all matter. This is especially important when using a community translation as evidence for a new theory.

The Traveler’s ability to understand a few phrases does not establish that every hilichurl can speak the same way or that all groups use identical expressions.

Why the language matters

Hilichurlian gives the game a way to show that hilichurls have communication and culture, rather than functioning only as enemies. The language research is part of the larger story about who is affected by the curse and how people choose to treat them.

Read language clues alongside Caribert, the hilichurl books, and the quests where the Traveler speaks with them. That keeps translation separate from assumptions about a whole people.
