Hilichurlian

Hilichurlian is the language recorded in Ella Musk’s field handbook, Jacob Musk’s poems, and the Traveler’s conversations with hilichurls. The game offers useful vocabulary clues, but meaning can depend on tone and context, and it has not supplied a complete grammar or word-for-word dictionary.

A language preserved in partial records

Hilichurlian is the language used by hilichurls in Genshin Impact. The game records it through Ella Musk’s phrasebook and commissions, Jacob Musk’s written songs, and exchanges in which the Traveler tries to understand hilichurls who are willing to communicate.

Those sources are fragments from different speakers and writers. They establish that the language carries meaning, but they do not amount to a full corpus, grammar, or dictionary.

What Ella’s handbook can translate

The Handy Handbook of Hilichurlian is a practical phrasebook Ella gives the Traveler during Mimi Tomo. It offers short glosses for greetings, preferences, food, colors, time, and directions, and repeatedly warns that its help is basic and still needs field testing.

The handbook shows why a one-word translation can be misleading. ‘Mita’ can refer to meat or something liked, while ‘gusha’ can name plants or something disliked; tone and the immediate situation help distinguish the sense. The book does not provide a complete set of rules for resolving every phrase.

Commissions test meaning in conversation

In Language Exchange and Poetry Exchange, Ella uses her notes to try to communicate with living hilichurls. Their reactions show that a phrase has to work in a social situation, not just match a word in a glossary. Ella is still learning, and one commission can branch into different outcomes depending on how the conversation goes.

Mimi Tomo makes the phrasebook an aid for a particular investigation: the Traveler interprets requests and clues from several hilichurls. The quest does not present Ella’s short glossary as a universal diplomatic protocol or a guarantee that every tribe speaks identically.

Songs are transcribed, not fully translated

Hilichurl Ballad Selection preserves seven songs written in Hilichurlian, along with Jacob Musk’s notes about when he heard them. The collection does not supply a literal English translation for every line, so a modern word-by-word gloss should be identified as an interpretation built from other evidence.

Ella’s Draft Hilichurl Ballads are different again: they are her own early compositions, not transcripts of an established hilichurl song tradition. Keep a phrasebook, a song Jacob collected, and a poem Ella wrote attributed to their own source.

The curse adds history, not a language family tree

Requiem of the Echoing Depths and Caribert connect hilichurl transformation with the Curse of the Wilderness and Khaenri’ah. They also show that the curse can affect memory, speech, and a person’s former identity. This history helps explain why human characters cannot simply converse with hilichurls in a common language.

The quests do not give Hilichurlian’s full origin or map it to a known language. It is safer to keep the established link between the curse and speech loss separate from theories about the language’s ancestry or how much of an earlier language survives in it.

The Version 7.2 Dragonspine scene

In A Yesterday Sent From Beyond, hilichurls build fortifications facing Dragonspine while the Skyfrost Nail’s power releases memories from the Ley Lines. Venti identifies the voices the Traveler hears as likely belonging to the people of Sal Vindagnyr, then suggests the hilichurls have gathered in response because the collapse of a homeland resonates with their own history.

This is Venti’s interpretation of one group, and it is not a conversation in Hilichurlian. The scene adds no new vocabulary or translation; it adds a story about how hilichurls may respond to remembered catastrophe.

What remains open

The game has not published a complete grammar, a reliable translation of every song, or a history of how Hilichurlian developed. It also has not shown that every tribe uses the same expressions with the same connotations.

Use the handbook for the glosses it actually gives, read each dialogue in its scene, and keep later or community translations labeled as interpretations. Hilichurlian is a real part of the game’s storytelling, but the available evidence remains partial.
