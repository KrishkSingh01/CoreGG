Pharmacist's Notebook (I)

The first of Yasumoto’s three journal entries records his grief after his mentor Naoko’s death and his helplessness as Tatarigami sickness spreads across Yashiori Island.

The first entry in Yasumoto’s notebook series

This quest item is one of three notes left by Yasumoto on Yashiori Island. The set is discovered through the hidden exploration objective Yasumoto’s Last Notes, which follows Treatment on the Island.

Notebook I is a brief entry that predates the later note about the Traveler helping Yasumoto. It provides the emotional context for the series rather than a full account of the later treatment research.

Naoko’s death and a spreading illness

Yasumoto writes that his mentor Naoko-sensei has died. He believes he is now the last traveling doctor left on the island during the Tatarigami outbreak, and he watches the remaining residents fall ill one after another.

The note gives no effective treatment at this stage. Yasumoto says he can only make sweet Lavender Melon soup for people and admits that pretending it is medicine is a form of self-deception.

Care when a cure is out of reach

Naoko taught that care can itself be a remedy. Yasumoto remembers the advice but doubts it, wondering if medical professionals repeat such words mainly to comfort themselves when they cannot help their patients.

That tension gives the small entry its purpose: Yasumoto is still trying to care for people, but he is honest about the limits of what he can do. The passage does not say he has found a cure or started testing Naku Weed.

Keep the three notes in sequence

Notebook II and Notebook III continue Yasumoto’s story, including his contact with a woman connected to the miners and, later, the Traveler’s assistance. The associated quest describes his request for Naku Weed and the investigation that follows.

Notebook I itself does not establish Yasumoto’s ultimate fate. Read it as the series’ opening record of loss and helplessness, and use the later notes and quest dialogue for subsequent events.
