Pharmacist's Notebook (III)

Yasumoto’s final notebook entry thanks the Traveler for helping his research and says he is going to the southern shoals of Yashiori Island to test a full dose of the medicine.

The Traveler’s help advances the research

Yasumoto recognizes that the Traveler and Paimon have arrived on Yashiori Island. He says the Traveler’s help has fast-tracked his research and thanks those who assisted him.

That is the direct link between the notebook and Treatment on the Island, where the Traveler gathers Naku Weed at Yasumoto’s request. The note does not provide a detailed lab record or describe a completed cure.

A planned full-dose test

Yasumoto says he will travel to the southern shoals of Yashiori Island to test the medicine again, this time with a full dose. The entry ends before that test takes place on the page.

The notebook confirms his plan, not its result. It does not say whether he took the dose, whether it worked, or what happened to him afterward.

The open end of Yasumoto’s story

Notebook III is the last of the three notes recovered through Yasumoto’s Last Notes. The hidden exploration objective points the Traveler to the southern shore and a treasure, but those gameplay discoveries do not turn the diary’s unfinished test into a narrated outcome.

The notes leave Yasumoto’s fate unresolved. Claims that he died from the medicine or cured the Tatarigami are interpretations rather than statements in this final entry.
