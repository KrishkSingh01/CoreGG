Records of a Changing Village

This Liyue quest record documents a village’s evacuation after traces of an evil god’s remains were found and recounts the disappearance of Shenhe and the deaths of her parents.

A migration notice from an abandoned village

The document is recovered during Bygones Times Like Dust Passing, an Archon Quest segment in The Crane Returns on the Wind. Its description says it records the villagers’ migration after traces of a god’s remains were found.

It is not a book about Qingce Village or a broad social history of Liyue. The item is a short record connected to the abandoned settlement on Mt. Tianheng.

The exorcist family’s disappearance

The notice says that a branch family of exorcists lived in the eastern part of the village. Over about two weeks, the mother died of illness, the young daughter went missing, and the father died by suicide in the yard.

The note also reports that traces of evil god remains had been found and that villagers were unsure if the danger was gone. It names neither the family nor the entity.

How the quest connects it to Shenhe

The companion Ragged Notebook gives the father’s perspective: his search for a way to save his wife, his decision to leave Shenhe in a cave as a sacrifice, and his later remorse. Yun Jin reads the two documents together and realizes the opera’s heroic volunteer story changed what happened to the child.

Cloud Retainer explains that she found Shenhe in the cave fighting the god’s remains and adopted her. The evil deity and the daughter’s identity are established by the wider quest, not by the migration notice’s text in isolation.

The record’s limits

This notice gives a compressed timeline and community warning. It does not explain the summoning book, state whether the remains were fully destroyed, or describe what happened to Shenhe after she disappeared.

Use the diary, Yun Jin’s conversation, and Cloud Retainer’s testimony for those details. Keeping the records separate shows how a public notice and a family diary preserve different parts of one event.
