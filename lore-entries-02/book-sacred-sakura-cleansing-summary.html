Sacred Sakura Cleansing Summary

This short Inazuma quest record is found at the abandoned shrine during the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. Paimon finds it difficult to understand, so Hanachirusato explains the five barrier sites and the periodic purification of miasma from the Sacred Sakura’s roots.

A quest record found at the abandoned shrine

The Traveler finds this record while investigating the abandoned shrine in Sacrificial Offering, part of the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual World Quest. Paimon reads it but says it is difficult to understand; Hanachirusato replies that it was written in the common tongue and explains the ritual herself.

The quest does not identify an author for the item. It is a source that prompts Hanachirusato’s explanation, rather than a readable step-by-step manual that independently documents the whole purification.

How the roots collect miasma

Hanachirusato explains that the Thunder Sakura can absorb filth in the earth, but the pollution accumulates in their roots instead of disappearing. If too much builds up, the trees can wither and the Sacred Sakura can be endangered.

The Thunder Sakura are branches of the central Sacred Sakura on Mt. Yougou, and their roots carry the pollution toward its central root system. The quest’s explanation supplies the context that Paimon could not make out from the written item alone.

Five barriers and a periodic major cleansing

Shrine maidens normally perform minor purifications at five sites on Narukami Island, setting barriers around the Thunder Sakura roots to contain the filth. The sites are Konda Village, the abandoned shrine, Chinju Forest, Araumi, and the coast near the Kamisato Estate.

Hanachirusato says the major cleansing is due once every 60 years. The Traveler breaks the five barriers, purges the defilement at each root, then goes with Hanachirusato into the depths of Mt. Yougou to destroy the Miasmic Tumor at the central roots.

Hanachirusato and the memory of Saiguu

After the tumor is destroyed, Hanachirusato reveals that she is filth condensed from the memory of Kitsune Saiguu. She is not simply Saiguu returned to life: the memory became part of the miasma the ritual must remove.

Because the ritual cleanses all of the accumulated filth, Hanachirusato dissipates when it is completed. Her explanation comes from the World Quest dialogue, not from the short written summary item alone.

What the item can and cannot establish

The item’s discovery, name, and place in the quest are clear, but the Traveler cannot use its wording to perform the entire ritual without help. The 60-year cycle, five sites, role of the barriers, and final purification are explained by Hanachirusato across the quest.

Keep the item text, Hanachirusato’s instructions, and her later identity disclosure as related but distinct sources. The record is a clue within a quest, not an independent account of every event in Saiguu’s life.
