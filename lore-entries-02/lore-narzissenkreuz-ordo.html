Narzissenkreuz Ordo

The Narzissenkreuz Ordo was a Fontaine research group whose members tried to protect people from a prophesied disaster. Its experiments and legacy are uncovered across Fontaine world quests.

A research group in Fontaine

The Narzissenkreuz Ordo was a group of Fontaine researchers who tried to find a way to protect people from the prophecy. Its history is spread across several world-quest chains, so players often learn the group’s story out of order.

Rene, Alain, Jakob, Mary-Ann, and others have different relationships to the Ordo. The group’s name should not be used as though every member agreed on the same method or accepted the same risks.

The prophecy and the experiment

Fontaine’s prophecy predicts a flood and a transformation of its people. The Ordo’s researchers attempted to understand the Primordial Sea and develop a response. Their work involved experiments that later became dangerous to the people they meant to save.

The quest line asks whether a good goal can justify methods that harm others. It does not present every researcher as a villain: the characters disagree, make mistakes, and carry consequences in different ways.

Following the quest chain

Ann of the Narzissenkreuz, Ancient Colors, and Unfinished Comedy each reveal a part of the history. Ann’s story uses fairy-tale language; Ancient Colors brings the Melusines into the Ordo’s past; Unfinished Comedy shows the results of the group’s work in Fontaine’s present.

Reading these quests in sequence makes the characters’ motivations easier to understand. A name or machine encountered in one part may not make sense until a later quest explains who built it.

What remains unsettled

The Ordo’s history leaves questions about memory, identity, and the limits of alchemy. Some details are told by characters whose memories are incomplete or whose accounts use fictional framing.

Treat confirmed quest events as the foundation. Interpretations about what the Ordo would have done next should stay labeled as theories.
