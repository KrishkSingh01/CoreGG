The Dragon King

Nibelung is identified in Teyvat’s lore as the Dragon King. The name belongs to the ancient history of the dragons and the conflict that shaped the world before the current nations.

The game reveals Nibelung through old written sources and newer story chapters. The timeline is incomplete, so a detailed biography cannot yet be written as if every event were directly witnessed.

The ancient war

Before Sun and Moon describes the Primordial One fighting the Seven Sovereigns. Other records connect Nibelung to the dragons’ resistance and the conflict with the Heavenly Principles.

Because these accounts come from different periods and narrators, they should be compared rather than merged into one perfect chronology. Later quests may confirm or revise how players understand the war.

Nibelung and the moons

Recent Song of the Welkin Moon developments connect Nibelung’s story to the Three Moons and the authority that shaped Teyvat. This makes older book passages newly important to the current plot.

A connection in a new quest does not automatically answer every question about who created the moons or why. Follow the scenes and the books together, and separate direct statements from proposed explanations.

What remains unknown

The game has not shown Nibelung’s full life, motives, or current state. The Dragon King’s role is established, but many details remain part of an unfolding story.

This entry will be updated as the game provides direct evidence. Until then, treat exact timelines and hidden identities as interpretation unless a source states them clearly.
