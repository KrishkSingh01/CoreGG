Primordial One

The Enkanomiyan book Before Sun and Moon describes the Primordial One’s war with the Seven Sovereigns and four Shades, and says it may have been Phanes. The game has not explicitly equated that book’s figure with the Heavenly Principles.

The Enkanomiyan source

Before Sun and Moon is a forbidden book preserved in Enkanomiya and collected as a volume of the Byakuyakoku Collection. Its description calls it a mixture of fables and histories from the world’s beginning through the creation of the Dainichi Mikoshi.

It is a scribe’s surviving account, not an omniscient narration. Orobashi banned the book after reading it, and later events explain that it held a history Celestia wanted hidden. Its importance does not remove the need to attribute claims to the source.

The first throne and the Four Shades

The section When the Doves Held Branches says the eternal throne of the heavens came and the world was made anew. It names the true lord as the Primordial One and says it fought seven dragon Sovereigns of the old world.

The same passage says the Primordial One created four shining Shades of itself. The book is the source for the names and basic account of these early events; it does not provide a complete chronology or independent confirmation from another narrator.

The second throne and Enkanomiya’s fall

The Funerary Year records that a second throne came and war began again. The account says the heavens collapsed and the Enkanomiyans’ ancestors fell into the underground realm that became Byakuyakoku.

The passage does not identify the second throne or explain its later identity. Keep it as the book’s account of the conflict rather than assigning it to Nibelung, the Second Descender, or another figure without a direct source.

Phanes is a tentative identification

The book includes the section On Phanes, or The Primordial One. Its narrator proposes that the Primordial One may have been Phanes and describes that being with wings and a crown, born from an egg and androgynous in nature.

The word may is part of the source. Version 7.2 adds one line in which the Tsaritsa calls the force behind her Trial of Usurpation Phanes, but that dialogue does not explicitly say that this is the same figure named in Before Sun and Moon.

The Heavenly Principles are not explicitly equated

The Fontaine Archon Quest says the Heavenly Principles took power from the ancient dragons, but does not name them as the Primordial One or Phanes. In Version 7.2, the Tsaritsa addresses Phanes while discussing the trial and the Holy Soil phenomenon; other characters describe those systems as the Heavenly Principles’ work.

These details make a connection plausible, but the game has not directly stated that the Enkanomiyan Primordial One, Phanes, the Heavenly Principles, and the First Descender are all one identity. Present that equation as an interpretation, not a confirmed fact.
