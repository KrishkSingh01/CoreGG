Prologue: Song of the Dragon and Freedom

The Prologue follows the Traveler’s first major journey in Mondstadt and the crisis surrounding Dvalin.

The Beginning of the Journey

The Prologue Archon Quest, 'Song of the Dragon and Freedom,' marks the foundational chapter of Genshin Impact, chronicling the Traveler's arrival in the nation of Mondstadt and their introduction to the politics, elemental laws, and hidden perils of Teyvat. Separated from their twin by the Sustainer of Heavenly Principles and awakening after a centuries-long slumber, the Traveler rescues Paimon and sets out on a journey to find the Seven Archons.

Upon arriving outside the gates of Mondstadt, the calm skies are violently shattered by Stormterror (Dvalin), who besieges the city with howling tempests. The Traveler assists Acting Grand Master Jean, Cavalry Captain Kaeya, and the Outrider Amber in repelling the assault, earning the honorary title of 'Honorary Knight' of the Knights of Favonius.

The Corruption of Dvalin and the Poison of Durin

As the mystery unfolds, the quest reveals that Dvalin is not a malicious monster, but one of the Four Winds of Mondstadt—the Dragon of the East—who was once Barbatos's dearest companion. Five hundred years ago, Dvalin defended Mondstadt against the abyssal shadow dragon Durin, slaying the beast above Dragonspine but accidentally swallowing its corrupted, toxic blood.

Poisoned by the abyssal venom and falling into a deep, centuries-long slumber, Dvalin awoke in the modern era to find that the mortals of Mondstadt had forgotten his heroic sacrifices and now feared him as a terrifying beast. Explaining this sorrow, the Abyss Order exploited Dvalin's agony, using Abyss Mages to whisper lies into his mind and binding him with blood clots to turn him into a weapon of total destruction against humanity.

The Holy Lyre der Himmel and the Assault on the Lair

To save the dragon without slaying him, Venti teams up with the Traveler, Jean, and Diluc. Together, they infiltrate the Fatui-guarded Goth Grand Hotel, retrieve the sacred Holy Lyre der Himmel from the cathedral vaults, and travel to the whispering cliffs to play the forgotten melodies of Barbatos.

When an Abyss Mage disrupts the meeting, the allies storm Stormterror's Lair—the ancient ruins of Old Mondstadt. Flying alongside Venti on the wind, the Traveler shatters the abyssal blood clots on Dvalin's neck during an aerial duel inside the storm barrier, purifying the dragon's body and restoring his sanity.

The Ambush of Signora and the Stolen Gnosis

The quest concludes with a bittersweet triumph: Dvalin rescues the heroes from the collapsing tower, reconciles with Barbatos, and soars into the open sky as a free guardian of the realm. However, as Venti and the Traveler exit Mondstadt Cathedral, they are violently ambushed by the Eighth Fatui Harbinger, La Signora.

Freezing Venti in ice, Signora mocks him as a weak, drunken bard and forcefully rips the Anemo Gnosis from his chest before escaping. This shocking climax established the core overarching conflict of the game: that behind the local troubles of each nation, the Fatui are executing a grand continental heist to strip the gods of their power. With Venti's guidance, the Traveler turns their gaze south toward Liyue, embarking on a long odyssey across the continent.
