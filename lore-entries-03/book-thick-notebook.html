Henry Morton’s Optional Quest Notes

Thick Notebook is a short Mondstadt quest item written by Henry Morton. It can be found at his camp south of the Thousand Winds Temple during the World Quest Time and Wind; it is not a library-held field journal or a long scientific diary.

The item has two brief notes. Henry sees the phrase “time and wind” and wonders whether it refers to the Anemo Archon; he then guesses that any clues may involve Anemo. Those are his working suspicions, not an explanation supplied by the notebook.

A Clue, Not the Quest’s Answer

The quest’s central investigation concerns the deserted island’s sundial and the unusual wind phenomenon there. Henry’s notes show how he initially interprets the phrase, but they do not document the island’s full history or identify its ancient builder.

The earlier claims about years of coastal ecology research, animal migration charts, Dragonspine weather readings, and a warning about the Spiral Abyss portal were not in the item text and have been removed.
