Verses of Equilibrium

This short Liyue devotional poem honors Rex Lapis and the yaksha adepti, recalling their demon-slaying and the yakshas’ departure without naming its author or date.

CanonReading progress · 0%Added to the archive 2026-11-18

A Quest-Item Offering to Rex Lapis

Verses of Equilibrium is a short Liyue quest item found near Wanwen Bookhouse. Its item description says it preserves ancient words used to honor Rex Lapis and the yaksha adepti, and records an older form of belief in the Geo Archon.

The poem praises Geo as enduring, associates Rex Lapis with law and wisdom, recalls his demon-slaying, and says the yakshas departed after fulfilling their duty. It ends as a lowly offering for the departed and a firelit prayer intended to please the Lord of Geo.

Read the Poem Within Its Limits

This is a devotional verse, not a treatise on elemental balance, Liyue’s ley lines, or geological engineering. It gives a compact view of how a worshipper commemorates Rex Lapis and the yakshas, but it does not name its author or date the poem.

The earlier claims about yin and yang, Geo resonance, agricultural qi, stone pillars as an energy grid, and scholarly or pharmacy readership were not present in the item and have been removed.
