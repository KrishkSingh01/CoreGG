The Fatui

The Fatui are Snezhnaya’s diplomatic, intelligence, and military organization, led by the Tsaritsa and her Eleven Harbingers; their stated aim is to challenge the Heavenly Principles, while their full plan remains hidden.

Snezhnaya’s State and Foreign Service

The Fatui are Snezhnaya’s diplomatic, intelligence, and military organization. Their envoys represent the Tsaritsa abroad, while agents, merchants, bankers, researchers, and soldiers carry out different parts of the state’s work. In the current account of Snezhnaya, the diplomatic mission operates from Zapolyarny Palace while the organization’s headquarters is the fortress of Glupov in Snezhnograd.

The Fatui have intervened in conflicts across Teyvat, and several of their operations have caused serious harm. The organization is not identical to every individual member’s motives: ordinary recruits can be workers who know little about the Harbingers’ plans.

The Tsaritsa, Harbingers, and Divisions

At the top is the Tsaritsa, Cryo Archon of Snezhnaya. The Eleven Fatui Harbingers are her executive officers, each using a commedia dell’arte codename and leading a division. Their authority is unusually broad, and their internal communications can be limited; rank, a division’s work, and the Fatui’s wider bureaucracy are related but not interchangeable.

The organization includes specialized groups such as the House of the Hearth, the Debt Collection division, and the Armory Palace. Chapter VII gives the House of the Hearth an agreement to become independent of the Fatui intelligence service, but the story does not specify when that change takes effect.

Delusions and the Rank-and-File

A Delusion is a power-granting device distinct from a Vision. The Tsaritsa gives Delusions to Harbingers and other elite Fatui, allowing their users to wield elemental power. The game shows that using one can exact a severe physical cost, as seen with Teppei in Inazuma; the details of how every Delusion is made are not fully established.

Fatui recruits include volunteers, people raised in the House of the Hearth, and people pulled into service through other circumstances. Their experiences range from homesickness to attachment to the places where they are stationed. The ordinary soldiers encountered by the Traveler do not necessarily know the full goals of their superiors.

Operations Across Teyvat

The Fatui’s foreign operations are a recurring source of conflict. In Mondstadt, Signora forcibly took Venti’s Gnosis. In Liyue, Childe’s plan used the possibility of releasing Osial to draw Rex Lapis out, putting the harbor at risk before the Qixing and Traveler stopped the threat. The Fatui later abandoned their Chasm expedition.

In Inazuma, Fatui agents supported the Vision Hunt Decree and supplied Delusions to Watatsumi’s resistance, worsening the conflict. In Sumeru, Dottore pursued the plan to turn Scaramouche into a god and negotiated with Nahida over the Dendro and Electro Gnoses. In Fontaine, Neuvillette later gave the Hydro Gnosis to Arlecchino. In Natlan, Capitano changed course from the Gnosis mission to the fight against the Abyss.

The Gnoses and the Tsaritsa’s Stated Aim

Pierro describes the Fatui’s broader target as the Heavenly Principles and says the Tsaritsa seeks to seize their authority, reconstruct the world, and free humanity from curses and lies. The Tsaritsa separately explains that Gnoses can amplify their bearers’ power and may allow them to exceed the limits of their authority. She does not disclose the complete plan or the price it will demand.

In the current Chapter VII account, the Pyro Gnosis is the only one whose location is unknown. This updates older summaries that described the Fatui as having secured every Gnosis or as preparing to start a fully explained final war.

Chapter VII: Projects and Current Status

The Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya chapters have changed the organization’s active leadership and exposed new projects. Project Stuzha consolidates resources for Project Mistilteinn, a weapons program built around energy reverse-engineered from Stellar Linchpins. The Archon Quest shows that the Curse of Death halted both this research and the weapon project.

After the Version 7.2 events in Celestia, the Tsaritsa says the Fatui’s remaining option is to fight. The exact design of the world she wants to build, the full role of the Gnoses, and the long-term outcome of the Fatui’s projects remain undisclosed.
