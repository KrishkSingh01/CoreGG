The Fatui

The Fatui are an organization based in Snezhnaya, with agents and diplomatic missions operating across Teyvat.

The Iron Vanguard of the Cryo Archon

The Fatui is the massive political, diplomatic, and military organization of Snezhnaya, serving directly under the command of the Cryo Archon, the Tsaritsa. Operating from the grand halls of Zapolyarny Palace, the Fatui maintains embassies, commercial fronts (such as the Northland Bank), and heavily armed military detachments in every nation across Teyvat. To ordinary citizens, they are feared as ruthless schemers, bullies, and conquerors.

Yet, within the ranks of the Fatui, their mission is viewed not as petty villainy, but as a tragic, necessary crusade. Founded by Pierro ('The Jester'), the first survivor of Khaenri'ah to swear fealty to the Tsaritsa after the Cataclysm, the Fatui exists for a singular cosmic purpose: to overthrow the tyranny of Celestia, burn away the stagnant old world, and liberate humanity from the oppressive gaze of the Heavenly Principles.

The Eleven Fatui Harbingers

At the pinnacle of the Fatui hierarchy stand the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, supreme commanders who have been granted god-like authority, specialized elemental foci known as Delusions, and rank according to their sheer destructive capability. From Pierro (Rank 1 / Director), Il Capitano (Rank 1), Il Dottore (Rank 2), and Columbina (Rank 3) to figures like Arlecchino (Rank 4), Tartaglia (Rank 11), and the deceased Signora (Rank 8), the top Harbingers possess power rivaling that of the Archons themselves.

Each Harbinger commands a distinct division within the empire: Dottore oversees clandestine biotechnology and mechanical experimentation; Pantalone manages continental finance, debt, and the Northland Bank; Arlecchino administers the House of the Hearth espionage network; and Capitano leads the indomitable frontline legions. Despite internal rivalries and conflicting philosophies, all Harbingers are united by their loyalty to the Tsaritsa's overarching vision.

Delusions and Abyssal Engineering

A cornerstone of Fatui military supremacy is the mass distribution of Delusions—artificial foci engineered from the crystallized remains of fallen ancient gods and Abyssal alchemy. While Delusions allow mortal soldiers to wield devastating elemental power without possessing a Vision, they take a horrifying toll on ordinary human vitality, rapidly draining the user's lifespan and inflicting agonizing elemental backlashes if overused.

The Fatui have repeatedly shown a willingness to sacrifice mortal lives and instigate regional wars to achieve their strategic goals. From supplying Delusions to the Sangonomiya resistance during Inazuma's civil war to attempting to drown Liyue Harbor by unsealing the god Osial, the Harbingers view collateral damage as an acceptable cost in the grand struggle against the divine order.

The Assembly of the Gnoses and the Final War

Through centuries of patient calculation, the Fatui have steadily stripped the Archons of their Gnoses. In Mondstadt, Signora ambushed Venti; in Liyue, Zhongli traded his Gnosis through a sovereign contract; in Inazuma, Yae Miko traded the Electro Gnosis to save the Traveler; in Sumeru, Nahida exchanged her Gnoses with Dottore for forbidden cosmic truths; and in Fontaine, Neuvillette granted the Hydro Gnosis to Arlecchino.

With nearly every piece of the celestial puzzle secured, the Fatui are preparing for their ultimate confrontation against the throne of heaven. Snezhnaya stands as a fortress nation on total war footing, ready to trigger an apocalyptic rebellion that will determine whether Teyvat remains bound by celestial design or seizes its own destiny.
