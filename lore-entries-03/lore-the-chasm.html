The Chasm

The Chasm layers Liyue’s mines over an ancient civilization, a Celestial Nail called the Spiritstone, and the Cataclysm-era battle sealed in the Chasm’s Bed.

Liyue’s Surface Mine and Underground Network

The Chasm is a western Liyue mining region with a surface basin and extensive Underground Mines. The name covers places with distinct histories: active and abandoned workings, ancient ruins, the Inverted City, the corrupted Spiritstone, and the space known as the Chasm’s Bed.

The Liyue Qixing closed the area after earthquakes, mining accidents, and dark ooze spread through the mines. The Chasm Delvers quest follows the exploration team, the Lumenstone Adjuvant, and the Traveler’s effort to purify the corrupted Spiritstone. The game does not identify a confirmed culprit for that corruption.

The Spiritstone and the Inverted City

The Spiritstone is a Celestial Nail deep in the Underground Mines; its appearance is compared in-game to the Skyfrost Nail at Dragonspine. Requiem of the Echoing Depths reveals an upside-down city and a water pool with unusual properties. Dainsleif judges the civilization behind the city to be older than Khaenri’ah and the Abyss Order, despite architectural similarities.

A quest record links the ancient ruins to destruction by the Spiritstone, but the full history and identity of their builders are not settled. Keep this deep-time event separate from the later impact said to have opened the mining basin and from the Cataclysm-era battle below.

The Cataclysm and the Chasm’s Bed

During the Cataclysm, an iron meteorite fell into the Chasm and monsters emerged. The Millelith fought to defend Liyue, while Yaksha Bosacius and the brothers Boyang and Rongzhao became part of the effort to seal the monsters in a space called the Chasm’s Bed. The Fantastic Compass was central to that seal, which required a mortal and an adeptus to commit themselves to it.

Perilous Trail returns to the site centuries later. Xiao learns that the nameless Yaksha from the battle was Bosacius, and the group encounters a memory shaped by the strange space. This Cataclysm story is distinct from the Spiritstone’s much older history.

Khaenri’ah, the Abyss Order, and Halfdan

In Requiem of the Echoing Depths, Dainsleif investigates the Abyss Order’s activity in the Inverted City. The Traveler’s sibling’s plan involves the ancient pool and the hilichurls, while Dainsleif warns that the proposed change would multiply suffering rather than lift the curse. Black Serpent Knight Halfdan sacrifices himself to stop the device long enough for the Traveler to defeat the Abyss Lector.

The quest reveals a specific Abyss Order operation in the Chasm; it does not make the ancient builders Khaenri’ahns. Their ruins predate Khaenri’ah according to Dainsleif’s reading, even though the Abyss Order later uses the city.

The Chasm in the Current Story

In Version 7.2, the Liyue trial connected to the Nails of Retribution is reported as having taken place in the Chasm. The narration associates the trial with a Mora coin and a mysterious adeptus; the dialogue itself does not name the participant.

The Chasm therefore holds several layers of history rather than one continuous catastrophe: an ancient civilization and the Spiritstone, a later event associated with the opening of the basin, and the Cataclysm-era invasion and seal. Quest records answer some questions while leaving the origins and relationships among these events open.
