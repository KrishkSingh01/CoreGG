The Chasm

The Chasm is a mining region in western Liyue with an underground network and a long connection to the Abyss. Its quests reveal the miners’ records and the effects of the dark mud.

Liyue above and below ground

The Chasm is a mining region in western Liyue with a surface area and a vast underground network. Its mines were an important source of resources, but the place also holds traces of ancient battles and unusual powers.

The surface and the Underground Mines have different histories. The Chasm Delvers quest line connects the workers’ present-day situation to what lies beneath the rock.

The dark mud and the Abyss

The dark mud is a dangerous substance that interferes with the Traveler’s exploration. The quests reveal that the underground region is connected to Abyssal forces and to a threat spreading through the mines.

The Chasm’s connection to the Abyss is not the same as the Abyss Order’s full plan. The location contains its own events, characters, and ancient machinery, which should be read in their own context.

Khaenri’ah and the Ruin Serpent

The underground story reveals traces of Khaenri’ah and the work carried out there. Ruin machines and the Ruin Serpent show how advanced devices can outlast the people who built them.

Requiem of the Echoing Depths connects the Traveler’s journey in the Chasm to Dainsleif and Khaenri’ah. It adds information but does not explain every mystery about the nation.

Why the location matters

The Chasm is a bridge between Liyue’s mining history and the larger story of Khaenri’ah. Its geology, workers, and ruins all contribute evidence.

Follow the quests before combining every symbol in the mines into a theory. The game gives several direct answers and leaves other questions unresolved.
