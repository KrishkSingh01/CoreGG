Song of the Welkin Moon

Song of the Welkin Moon is the Archon Quest chapter spanning a Prelude and ten acts, from Natlan to Nod-Krai and Sumeru. Through Version 6.6 it resolves the Wild Hunt and Columbina’s lunar story while leaving the Traveler’s sibling and several cosmological questions open.

An Archon Quest chapter, not the daily blessing

Song of the Welkin Moon is the title of the Archon Quest chapter that opens in Natlan and takes the Traveler to Nod-Krai. The Prelude arrived in Version 5.8, Acts I–VIII continued through Nod-Krai, and Acts IX–X returned to Sumeru in Version 6.6.

The chapter title shares the words “Welkin Moon” with the daily Blessing of the Welkin Moon, but the quest record is about the Traveler’s story. The earlier page’s claims about the subscription icon, Primogems, or a confirmed connection to the Three Moon Sisters were unsupported and have been removed.

Three story strands converge

The Nod-Krai story braids three threads: Rerir and the Wild Hunt; Columbina and the Three Moon Goddesses; and the Traveler’s origin and sibling. The Prelude begins in Natlan and ends with Varka’s invitation to Nod-Krai. In Act I, the Traveler recognizes the landing site of their spaceship and encounters the Frostmoon Scions and their lunar traditions.

The acts gradually connect these questions instead of answering them all at once. The memory of the crash and the sibling’s presence outside the ship complicate the account the Traveler previously knew; the chapter does not fully explain the sibling’s later path.

Rerir and the Wild Hunt

The first conflict reveals that Rerir, a Khaenri’ahn and one of the Five Sinners, is behind the Wild Hunt. The Hunt is tied to his own scattered body and its reconstruction, not simply an unrelated group of monsters. His history with Tholindis and the Crimson Moon descendants gives the conflict a personal Khaenri’ahn dimension.

Acts III and IV bring Dainsleif into the history of the Five Sinners and the events that preceded the Cataclysm. Rerir’s strand reaches its immediate resolution through the Moon Gate, though the wider history of Khaenri’ah and the Abyss remains part of the main story beyond this chapter.

Columbina and the Three Moons

The Frostmoon Scions’ tradition remembers a time when Teyvat had three moons and three Moon Goddesses. Later acts add accounts from Nicole and the moons themselves: the sisters are identified as Sonnet, Canon, and Aria, and their imprisonment and role in the old war are shown through quest revelations.

Columbina is connected to the Frost Moon’s legacy, not simply revealed as one of the original sisters. In Act VIII the sisters pass their authorities to her, and she emerges as the Trilune Goddess. This resolves the chapter’s central question about her lunar identity while leaving other cosmological disputes open.

The Traveler reaches the old ship

The chapter repeatedly returns to the Traveler and sibling’s arrival. The Traveler recognizes Nod-Krai as the spaceship’s landing site; later memories show the sibling outside the ship during the crash, at odds with the player-character’s earlier understanding of their shared arrival.

The story eventually reaches the ship and attributes its placement to the Heavenly Principles. It does not resolve every inconsistency in the twins’ recollections, classify the sibling as another Descender, or explain the sibling’s complete route through Teyvat.

Sumeru, Irminsul, and the older world

Acts IX and X move the chapter to Sumeru. Nahida’s illness and a crisis in Irminsul draw in Collei, Tighnari, the Akademiya, and figures tied to the older gods. Apep’s account places Irminsul and the Ley Lines before the Heavenly Principles arrived and describes the older Light Realm power that once flowed through the Ley Lines.

Act X reveals Dottore’s remaining involvement in the Irminsul crisis. Nahida uses the Pyro Gnosis in the plan to burn the corrupted Irminsul; Dottore dies, and Aaru takes on Irminsul’s role. The chapter resolves that immediate threat, while questions about the Seven Sovereigns’ return and the world’s oldest cosmology remain open.

What this chapter does and does not settle

By the end of Act X, the Wild Hunt’s immediate threat has ended, Columbina has received the Three Moon Goddesses’ authority, and the Sumeru crisis has been addressed. These are major story resolutions, not proof that every ancient legend or community theory about a failed fourth moon, the Gnoses, or the Traveler’s sibling is correct.

Keep direct quest revelations separate from Frostmoon Scion tradition, older book accounts, and interpretations that the game does not state outright. Chapter VII in Snezhnaya continues the main story after Song of the Welkin Moon; its later events should not be backfilled into this chapter’s Version 6.6 endpoint.
