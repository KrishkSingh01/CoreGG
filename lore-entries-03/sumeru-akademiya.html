The Sumeru Akademiya

The Sumeru Akademiya is the nation’s university and governing institution, organized into six Darshans; after the Sages’ coup, it reformed under new leadership and later restored the Akasha in an altered form.

Sumeru’s University and Governing Body

The Sumeru Akademiya is both the nation’s main governing institution and Teyvat’s most prestigious university. Founded by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, it predates the Cataclysm and remains central to Sumeru’s administration and scholarship.

It is not synonymous with Sumeru: forest villages, the Forest Watchers, the Eremites, and the Temple of Silence all exist outside its institutional structure.

The Six Darshans and Civil Administration

The Akademiya’s Six Darshans preserve the six Great Schools established at its founding. Each is headed by a Great Sage, with one Sage serving as Grand Sage. The six disciplines include biology, elemental study, linguistics, history, astrology, and technology.

Alongside the Sages, civil offices handle administration, trade, bazaar affairs, and discipline. The Matra, led by the General Mahamatra, investigate serious academic offenses; the Mahamata are civil officials. The Akademiya’s academic leadership and its everyday service bodies are related, but they are not interchangeable roles.

The Akasha System and Its Abuse

Rukkhadevata created the Akasha System as a network for sharing knowledge drawn from the people’s wisdom. Under the Sages, it became a tool for controlling information and collecting citizens’ dreams to support their attempt to create a new god.

Four Sages conspired with the Fatui Harbinger Il Dottore and imprisoned Nahida. The plan to use the Akasha and the people’s dreams was defeated during the Sumeru Archon Quest; the corrupt Grand Sage Azar was removed, and Nahida shut the system down.

Reform and Current Leadership

After the coup, Alhaitham served temporarily as Acting Grand Sage while the institution reorganized. He has since stepped down; Naphis, the Sage of Amurta, now holds the Grand Sage seat. Nahida is Sumeru’s Dendro Archon and oversees reform, but she is not the Grand Sage or the day-to-day administrator of the university.

The Sages’ conspiracy does not mean that every scholar or all six Darshans supported it. The game distinguishes the leaders who colluded from other scholars and officials who continued their work or opposed the plot.

The Akasha After Its Shutdown

The Akademiya shut down the old Akasha after the Sumeru Archon Quest; it was not simply destroyed. Later story developments restore it in an altered form to connect with Irminsul, and Version 7.2 shows the network active for Sumeru’s Hallowing response.

The current system and leadership should be kept separate from the former Sages’ scheme: the story presents the renewed network as a coordinated response to the crisis, while leaving its long-term shape open.
