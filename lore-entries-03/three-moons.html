The Three Moons

The Three Moons are the goddesses Sonnet, Canon, and Aria, tied to the Iridescent, Frost, and Eternal Moons. Later Archon Quests name the sisters, return the surviving Frost Moon, and gather their remaining lunar authority into Columbina.

Three Goddesses and Three Moons

Later Song of the Welkin Moon quests identify the Three Moons as three goddesses: Sonnet of the Iridescent Moon, Canon of the Frost Moon, and Aria of the Eternal Moon. The sisters and their moons belong to Teyvat’s pre-Archon history. New quest scenes give them names and roles that older books only approached through poetry and myth.

Records in the current story say the Dragon King Nibelung raised the moons to govern the celestial cycles in his absence. They were built with ancient technology and powered by kuuvahki, an energy older than the seven present-day elements. The moons were more than lights in the sky: the quests describe their goddesses as wielders of the combined authorities of Life, Death, Time, and Space.

The War and What Remains

The War of Funerary Flame brought the sisters’ story to catastrophe. The Iridescent Moon and the Eternal Moon were lost; the game now links Sonnet’s moon to the Crimson Moon after it fused with Abyssal power. Canon’s Frost Moon survived beyond the false sky rather than being destroyed with the others.

In Song of the Welkin Moon: Act VIII, the Frost Moon returns inside Teyvat’s sky and the Lunar Palace names the sisters directly. The sisters’ remaining lunar authority is gathered into Columbina, who is revealed as the Trilune Goddess. The other two moons remain absent; the story has not established that they can be restored.

Read Older Moon Stories as Older Sources

Books such as Moonlit Bamboo Forest and scattered records preserve earlier versions of moon lore, but they are not interchangeable with the later Archon Quest scenes. A folk tale can echo a real event without supplying a complete timeline, and similarities between moon imagery, Seelie history, or other civilizations do not by themselves identify the same people or event.

The strongest current account comes from the Song of the Welkin Moon quests and the Frostmoon Scions’ records. The game has clarified the sisters’ names and the Frost Moon’s return while leaving the recovery of the other two moons unresolved.
