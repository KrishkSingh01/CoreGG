The Three Moons

The Three Moons are a major subject of Teyvat’s ancient stories and the current Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest. Older books preserve poetic versions of their past; newer quest events add direct developments, so a folktale should not be treated as a complete record.

The Celestial Maidens of the Primordial Sky

In the dawn of time, before the Archon War, before the Seven, and before humanity was divided into separate nations, the night sky of Teyvat was illuminated not by a solitary moon, but by Three Bright Moons. Their names were Aria, Sonnet, and Canon—three sisters who shared the sovereignty of the night and lived together in grand celestial palaces high above the clouds.

The Three Sisters represented the harmonious passage of time and lunar cycles. They traveled across the heavens in silver chariots, taking turns to govern the stars, while receiving the devotion of the ancient Seelie race, who served as their emissaries and palace guides. During this primordial golden age, the world enjoyed warm, silver moonlight that nurtured ancient forests and blessed humanity with perpetual peace.

The Cosmic Calamity and the Fall of the Sisters

The golden age ended in an apocalyptic cosmic catastrophe known as the 'Great Calamity of Heaven and Earth.' According to the ancient allegorical chronicle 'Moonlit Bamboo Forest' and the research notes of Enkanomiya, an incomprehensible cosmic war erupted between heaven and an outer entity (often associated with the Second Who Came).

The strife tore through the celestial firmament, shattering the grand silver palaces and turning the Three Sisters against one another in madness and grief. In the cataclysm that followed, two of the moons were completely pulverized, their crystalline fragments raining down onto Teyvat and leaving deep craters in the earth. Only one sister's cold, lifeless corpse remained suspended in the night sky—the pale moon that mortals gaze upon today.

The Crimson Moon and the Welkin Moon

In addition to the pale moon of modern Teyvat, ancient lore records the terrifying presence of the 'Crimson Moon.' Associated with the first dynasty of Khaenri'ah and the ancient curse of the House of the Hearth, the Crimson Moon represented an eclipsed, bloody lunar cycle that watched over underground civilizations when the false sky dimmed.

Conversely, the gentle 'Welkin Moon'—celebrated by travelers for its daily blessings—recalls the primordial warmth of the fallen lunar sisters. The motifs of the three moons appear in ancient architecture across the continent: the triple-crescent symbols carved into Domain gateways, the Triquetra emblems on ancient pillars, and the lunar motifs worn by the Frostmoon Scions.

The Mystery of the Surviving Lunar Lore

While modern mortals treat the Three Moons as quaint fairy tales, the Fatui and the Hexenzirkel study them with immense seriousness. The destruction of the moons is closely tied to the fall of the Seelie race, who withered into mindless wisps after being cast out from the celestial court.

The Three Moons stand as a poignant symbol of lost perfection: a reminder that the current cosmological order of Teyvat is built on the ruins of a far grander, older universe that was shattered by war.
