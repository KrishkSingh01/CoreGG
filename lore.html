Sources & references

CoreGG’s reading guides are original explanations, not full reproductions of HoYoverse articles or in-game books. The Genshin Impact Wiki helps locate and categorize records; quests, books, character stories, and item descriptions remain the primary lore evidence.

Confirmed story details are labeled Canon. Reading between the lines is labeled Interpretation. Fan speculation is labeled Theory and is never presented as confirmed lore.
