Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how advertising and external resources on CoreGG may use information when you visit pages on this website.

Advertising partners

CoreGG uses Google AdSense and Monetag to display advertising. These providers and their advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, limit repeated ads, help prevent invalid activity, and, where permitted by your settings and applicable rules, personalize ads using visits to this or other websites.

When an ad tag or ad is requested, an advertising provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the page being viewed, and interactions with an ad. The providers handle this information under their own policies and settings.

Google explains its advertising cookies in its advertising technologies policy. Monetag explains its practices in its Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

Your advertising and cookie choices

  • Manage Google's ad personalization in Google Ads Settings.
  • Use Monetag's advertising opt-out for the formats covered by its tool.
  • You can use your browser settings to block or clear cookies. Some advertising preferences may need to be set separately in each browser or device.

Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. Ad personalization and availability can depend on your region and consent choices.

External images and resources

Some CoreGG pages load game images or other resources from external providers. When your browser requests those files, the provider may receive technical request information. Those providers' own privacy policies apply to their services.
