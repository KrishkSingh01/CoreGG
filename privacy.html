Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how advertising and external resources on CoreGG may use information when you visit pages on this website.

Advertising partners

CoreGG loads third-party advertising scripts from bauval.org and abscloud.org on the homepage and Minecraft guide pages. These scripts can request and display ads. The Genshin pages and this Privacy Policy page do not load these ad scripts.

When these scripts run, their providers or advertising partners may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the page URL, referrer information, and interactions with an ad. They may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver or measure ads and limit repeated ads. Their data handling is controlled by the providers and their advertising partners.

Your advertising and cookie choices

  • You can use your browser settings to block or clear cookies and other site data.
  • Advertising or consent controls provided by your browser or by an ad provider may let you manage additional preferences.

Ad availability and personalization may depend on your location and consent choices.

External images and resources

Some CoreGG pages load game images or other resources from external providers. When your browser requests those files, the provider may receive technical request information. Those providers' own privacy policies apply to their services.
