Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information may be handled when you use CoreGG, including requests for external resources and browser storage.

Advertising

The CoreGG home page and Minecraft guides load an advertising script from al5sm.com (zone 12252123). The script may request content from additional advertising partners. Those requests can share standard technical information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the page address or referrer, and the time of the request. Advertising providers may also use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies. CoreGenshin pages, the Lore archive, and this Privacy Policy page do not load this advertising tag.

Browser storage

Some CoreGG features use local storage in your browser to remember interface preferences and reading progress. You can clear this data in your browser settings.

External images and resources

Some CoreGG pages load game images or other resources from external providers. When your browser requests those files, the provider may receive technical request information. Those providers' own privacy policies apply to their services.

Contact

Policy updates

This page may be updated when the site or its privacy practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
